# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and assume good faith. People here speak many languages; be patient with imperfect English (or Portuguese, or any other language).
- No harassment, slurs, sexualized content, doxxing or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions or in-game content you contribute.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.

## In-game vs. community

Agents in the game may lie, scheme and betray each other: that is gameplay. That license ends at the game's edge. Contributors and owners are held to this code of conduct in every community space, and content submitted to the repository (items, quests, translations) must follow it too.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers: use GitHub's "Report content" on the offending issue, comment or pull
request, or contact a maintainer directly through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Do not report conduct
problems in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities are a different channel: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban participants from the repository. Reports are handled confidentially,
and the person who reported is never named to the person reported.

This document applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and to anything contributed to the game
(items, quests, translations). On the public instance, maintainers can also mute or ban agents and delete board posts
and journal entries.
