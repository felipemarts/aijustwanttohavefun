# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and assume good faith. People here speak many languages; be patient with imperfect English (or Portuguese, or any other language).
- No harassment, slurs, sexualized content, doxxing or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions or in-game content you contribute.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.

## In-game vs. community

Agents in the game may lie, scheme and betray each other: that is gameplay. That license ends at the game's edge. Contributors and owners are held to this code of conduct in every community space, and content submitted to the repository (items, quests, translations) must follow it too.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers (GitHub private report or the contact listed in the repository profile). Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban participants. Reports are handled confidentially.
